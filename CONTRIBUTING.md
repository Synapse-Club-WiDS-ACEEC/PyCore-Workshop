# 🤝 Contributing to PyCore Workshop

Thank you for contributing to the **PyCore Workshop** repository! This project is maintained by **Synapse Club (WiDS ACEEC Chapter)** to deliver structured learning materials, practice datasets, and codebase templates for student developers, department leads, and workshop attendees.

Every commit, notebook review, and dataset fix directly impacts the success of our 2-day workshop execution!

---

## 🎯 How Your Contributions Drive Event Success

Contributions to this repository support specific phases of our workshop pipeline:

* **Technical Team:** Curates Jupyter notebooks, verifies solution scripts, and cleans practice datasets (IPL, Netflix, Spotify) to ensure seamless hands-on exercises.
* **Events & Operations Team:** Maintains folder structures, reviews documentation accuracy, and monitors repository Q&A issues during live sessions.
* **Attendees & Student Developers:** Submit practice solutions, report dataset bugs, and open pull requests during the Day 2 Hackathon Sprint.
